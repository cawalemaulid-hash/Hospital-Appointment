ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Making Healthcare Access Easier

The aim of the Garissa Hospital Appointment System is to simplify the process of booking hospital appointments through an online platform.

This project is designed to help patients submit appointment requests, provide their details, select a doctor or department, and manage their bookings more conveniently.

By applying web technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the project demonstrates how technology can help solve everyday healthcare scheduling challenges.
